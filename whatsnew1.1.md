New: iPhone Duo support

Unfold your iPhone Duo and the app uses the big-screen layout: the ATC list and the live map side by side in landscape, and a full-screen map with a slide-in list in portrait. The station info card is sized for the Duo, follows the shape of the screen, and lets you scroll through long ATIS messages.

New: Contact Support

Tap the question mark next to the search field and choose Contact Support to reach us with a bug report, a feature request or a question.

The support page opens in your browser with the app version and your device already filled in, so we can help you faster. You write your message and send it yourself; the app sends nothing on its own.
